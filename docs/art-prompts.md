# Prompty zasobów graficznych

Wszystkie pięć zasobów utworzono wbudowanym ImageGen. Pliki źródłowe PNG zostały przekonwertowane do WebP bez dodatkowych zmian rysunku. Warianty zimowy, pustynny i wulkaniczny powstały przez edycję źródłowej panoramy jeziora.

## client/public/assets/lake-painted.webp

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background panorama for a mobile 2D side-view artillery game, no UI. Create a very wide 1536x768 landscape illustration matching a mature hand-painted cartoon game: soft clear pale blue sky occupying upper 45%, a simple flat warm yellow sun at upper right, a few restrained flat pale clouds. Behind: several layers of blue mountain silhouettes with atmospheric perspective, a quiet blue lake and small distant rocky islands, a tiny medieval ruined castle silhouette on the far right. Cool blue muted palette, simplified broad shapes, delicate painted texture, gentle light. All mountains and distant forests have LOW contrast and no black outlines. The entire bottom 30% should be calm open pale blue lake and very soft blue hills, without ANY foreground. Horizons at around 55%. This is BACKGROUND ONLY: no walkable brown land, no grassy foreground platforms, no characters, no worms, no weapons, no crates, no trees in front, no UI, no text. No detailed snowy peaks, no photorealism, no 3D render, no volumetric glow, no rays, no dramatic lighting, no saturated vegetation. Clean readable restrained artwork that will sit behind richly textured dark brown terrain and small colored cartoon worms.
```

## client/public/assets/soil-painted.webp

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production seamless tile texture for the exposed soil cross-section of a mature illustrated 2D artillery game. Square 1024x1024. Fill every pixel with continuous warm earthy brown soil, medium umber sienna (#805738 approx), with soft organic large painted variations. Embedded in earth are SPARSE individual irregular small charcoal and grey-brown rocks, a few medium angular stones with subtly painted facets, tiny scattered ochre pebbles, some compact dirt patches. Rock coverage no more than 12 percent. Mature hand-painted cartoon game art, subtle organic brush texture, simple readable shapes, not photorealistic, not noisy. There must be NO horizontal strata or straight lines, NO brick arrangement, NO grass, NO surface ground outline, NO sky, NO water, NO characters, NO objects, NO interface. This is an all-over soil material to mask with dynamically destructible terrain. Left/right and top/bottom edges should match for seamless tiling. No cracks across whole image, no glowing effects, no glossy rendering, no checkerboard, no transparent areas, no symbols, no text. Broad flat warm earthy shapes with restrained matte texture; individual embedded grey stones outlined with soft dark brown contours, visually like a cutaway of soil in a polished mobile Worms-style game.
```

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production BACKGROUND ONLY for a mobile 2D artillery game. Edit this panorama into the requested biome while preserving its wide 2:1 composition, quiet negative space, broad mature illustrated shapes, restrained soft brush texture and LOW contrast atmospheric perspective. Upper 45% is simple sky. Bottom 30% is very quiet open distance with no foreground. No walkable land, no outlined foreground trees, no characters, no worms, no weapons, no props, no UI, no text. No photorealism, dramatic clouds, lens effects, rays, bloom or 3D rendering. Gameplay terrain and colored worms will be drawn over this image. Small distant ruined castle on far right is retained. Soft blue palette, snow is cool pale blue not bright white. Mountain detail is extremely subdued.
```
